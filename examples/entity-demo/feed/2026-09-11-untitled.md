A post with no title, so its first line is what a reader sees.

An embed's fallback is mandatory and non-empty — that is what makes a post
legible to a reader who has no renderer for its payload — so with no `title` in
the frontmatter the ingest takes the first non-empty line of the body instead.
Write that line so it can stand alone.
