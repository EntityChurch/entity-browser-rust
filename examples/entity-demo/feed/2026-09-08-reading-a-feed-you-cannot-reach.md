title = "Reading a feed you cannot reach"
created_at = 2026-09-08T11:45:00Z
+++

If a publisher's own origin is down — or was never yours to reach — somebody
else who already holds their posts can carry them. The carried entries stay at
their author's addresses and keep their author's signatures; the gatherer signs
only the record that says *I am carrying these*.

That is the whole of why a mirror is not a republication. The bytes are still
theirs, the attribution is still theirs, and a reader can check both without
asking anyone's permission.
